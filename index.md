# TravMiz Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 24, 2026**

TravMiz ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the TravMiz mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use TravMiz, we may collect:

- **Account information:** Name, email address, and phone number when you register or update your profile.
- **Profile information:** Profile pictures you choose to upload.
- **Saved information:** Destinations, hotels, homestays, restaurants, and other places you save.
- **Trip information:** Travel plans and related details you choose to enter.
- **Technical information:** Information necessary for the app to operate securely and reliably.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Display and update your profile.
- Save your favourite destinations and places.
- Provide travel discovery and trip-planning features.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
- Respond to support requests and protect the security of our services.

## 3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

TravMiz uses Supabase for backend services, including authentication, database storage, and image storage.

Information submitted through the app may be processed and stored by Supabase in accordance with its privacy and security practices.

When you choose to open external map services or communication applications, those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

We do not control the privacy practices of external services.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate and maintain TravMiz, or when required by applicable law.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored through TravMiz. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet applicable legal obligations.

If you wish to request deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact us using the email address below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

## 7. Children's Privacy

TravMiz is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
